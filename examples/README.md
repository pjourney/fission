# Machined plate

Open `machined-plate.FCStd` in Fission. This editable parametric design contains
a fully constrained rectangular sketch, Extrude, corner Fillet, a face-attached
hole sketch, Hole, and a three-hole Linear Pattern. The saved plate is 70 × 40 ×
12 mm with 4 mm holes and 1.5 mm corner fillets.

Double-click a feature in the Timeline to edit its native parameters. Double-click
the first sketch to change its dimensions; finish editing to recompute the later
features. Use Ctrl+Z to undo, F6 to fit, middle drag to pan, and Shift+middle drag
to orbit. Save a copy to keep this example intact.
